Motion graphics and tools

Step 6

Engagement design

  • First 3 seconds: his face at 1.30×, his voice from frame one, and a single line of text that opens a question. No logo sting or intro.
  • Watch time: a visible two-part structure (Syariah, then Teknikal) gives viewers a map. Zoom changes about every 4–6 s keep pace without effects.
  • Completion: the hook's open question pays off at 0:48, and the climax line and “Jaga-jagalah ya.” land right before the end card, so there is no long tail.
  • Shares: practical value for anyone with a food business. The end card asks people to send it to one.
  • Comments: an opinion question rather than “like and follow”: “Pada pendapat anda, kesalahan mana paling kerap berlaku?”
  • B-roll: none is used, because the source has none and I won't generate fake kitchen or factory footage. Where a shot lists optional B-roll, it slots in only if you supply real clips.
